This is the whole deck - both sheets, in and out of a batch. Known flags writes FALSE over every TRUE in the workbook and hands every batch back the words it had cleared. Seen counts only forgets how often each entry has come up, so the next pass comes in a fresh order; it never touches the workbook. Batch numbers and batch membership are left alone either way.

Flags is separate on purpose and Both leaves it alone: a flag is a note to yourself to come back and write with that word, not a record of progress, so throwing your progress out is no reason to throw the list away. It never touches the workbook.
